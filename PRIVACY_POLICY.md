# Privacy Policy for Blitz Math

**Effective Date:** July 21, 2026

Blitz Math ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application Blitz Math.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Identifiers & Device Data:** We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect Advertising ID (IDFA), IP address, and basic device metrics for ad personalization and analytics.
- **Game Data:** Player scores, levels, and achievements are stored locally and via Firebase services.

## 2. Tracking & Advertising
We request tracking permission via App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to show relevant ads via Google AdMob. If you decline, non-personalized ads will be shown.

## 3. Data Protection
We do not sell personal data to third parties. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is handled securely in compliance with applicable privacy laws.
